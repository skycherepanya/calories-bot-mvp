# Lifecore icon concepts

Date: 2026-11-12. Status: Selected and installed.

The **Core** icon concept was selected for production integration. It was deterministically resized to 1024×1024 (`AppIcon-1024.png`), added to `ios/Lifecore/Lifecore/Lifecore/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/Contents.json`, built for iOS Simulator (iPhone 17 / iOS 27.0), and visually verified on the simulator Home Screen (`verification/home-screen-icon.png`).

- [Core](lifecore-core.png): **Selected and installed**. Open protective loop and seed; calm native iOS identity.
- [Rhythm](lifecore-rhythm.png): flowing organic ribbon; alternative exploration.
- [Step](lifecore-step.png): ascending compact forms; alternative exploration.

## Generation prompts

### core

```text
Use case: logo-brand. Asset: a standalone premium native iOS app icon concept for Lifecore, a personal nutrition, recovery and athletic progress app. Brand direction: calm, inviting, precise, personally meaningful; a beautiful icon someone wants on their home screen. Output one square full-bleed 1024x1024 opaque icon artwork, NOT a presentation board or mockup. Background extends to all four square corners, no pre-rounded outer corners. Centered large distinctive symbol occupying about 60% of the canvas, generous optical margins. Exceptionally refined geometry and optical balance, crisp vector-like edges, restrained flat colors; readable at 48px. No text, letters other than specifically described abstract monogram, captions, gradients, shadows, glossy 3D, devices, watermark, ECG, medical cross, dumbbell, generic heart or stock fitness badges. Do not imitate an existing brand logo. Concept CORE: near-black forest-green background, warm luminous pale-mint symbol. A single broad organic C-shaped protective loop enclosing a small solid off-center rounded seed; the outer form feels like a smooth asymmetric river pebble with a deliberate opening at upper right. A bold unmistakable silhouette with broad negative space and a subtly uplifting orientation. Two solid shapes only. Quiet vitality, care for oneself, contemporary Swiss-meets-organic identity. Neither a power button nor a circular progress chart.
```
